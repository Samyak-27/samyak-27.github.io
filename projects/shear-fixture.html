Fabrication and inspection

Shear fixture manufacturing review

I checked machined parts for an asphalt shear-test fixture against the submitted CAD and secured a supplier agreement to remake two base plates.

Organization
UC Pavement Research Center
Scope
Machined asphalt shear-fixture components
Outcome
Supplier agreed to remake two base plates

Checking the manufactured parts

I reviewed the delivered components against the CAD drawings and checked how the pieces fit together. The existing rings fit, while the base plate review revealed a drilling discrepancy.

Working through the discrepancy

I met with the machining supplier to compare the delivered parts, the submitted drawings, and the intended features. The review identified which pieces could be retained and gave the supplier a drawing reference for the corrective work.

Agreeing on the correction

The supplier agreed to remake two base plates to match the submitted CAD. The agreement defined the scope of the rework while retaining the rings that had passed the fit check.
