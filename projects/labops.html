LabOps service portal

An internal service portal that gives laboratory staff one place to report equipment issues, assign work, and preserve repair records.

View the public starter on GitHub
Organization
UC Pavement Research Center
Interface
React, TypeScript
Backend
Supabase Auth, Cloudflare D1

The workflow

A service request begins with enough context for someone else to act on it. In LabOps, staff can describe an issue, identify its location on a facility map, and attach relevant photos or documents. An assignee can track the request and record the repair or resolution.

What I built

I developed the request, assignment, and repair workflows in React and TypeScript. Supabase Auth handles sign-in, and Cloudflare D1 stores the application records. I added map-based reporting and searchable histories so staff could find earlier requests and their resolutions.

How it evolved

I iterated on the portal with feedback from research staff. The project grew from issue intake into a place to follow work through to completion and refer back to equipment service history.
