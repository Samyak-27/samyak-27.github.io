Equipment inventory & calibration

A searchable inventory that connects lab equipment to maintenance schedules, calibration certificates, and the records staff need to plan service.

Organization
UC Pavement Research Center
Data
Equipment records, maintenance tasks, calibration PDFs
Infrastructure
SQLite archive on Windows

Matching certificates to equipment

An inventory entry had to lead to the certificate for that specific instrument. I used serial numbers, asset tags, and source documents to link records. I kept ambiguous matches available for review and tracked missing calibration dates as gaps in the record.

Supporting maintenance workflows

I built search, grouped filters, room-based views, and certificate downloads. I added bulk maintenance updates and task completion tracking so staff could work through a calibration service list, then export selected records to CSV.

Keeping records available

I organized the equipment data and document archive in SQLite on a Windows host. I configured automatic startup, checked that the backup could be restored, and verified database and PDF access after a reboot.
