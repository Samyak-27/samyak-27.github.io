Research workstation data migration

I transferred more than 164,000 files from a laboratory computer backup and verified the transfer using its copy log.

Organization
UC Pavement Research Center
Transfer size
Approximately 45 GB
Tools
PowerShell, Robocopy

Moving the research files

The source was a backup of a laboratory workstation. I used PowerShell and Robocopy to transfer the files and retain a record of what copied, making the outcome easier to review across a large set of files.

Reading the copy results

The transfer covered 164,437 files, totaling about 45 GB. I reviewed the Robocopy log after the run and verified that it reported zero copy failures.
