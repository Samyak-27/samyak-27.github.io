Computer graphics

3D rendering engine

I built an interactive WebGL2 renderer that loads OBJ models, positions them in a scene, and shades their surfaces in the browser.

Platform
JavaScript, WebGL2
Model input
OBJ, vertex and normal buffers
Lighting
Per-fragment Phong shading

Organizing the scene

I built a scene graph to organize objects and their transformations, with camera controls to change the view. That let the renderer manage where objects sit in the scene independently of the viewer’s perspective.

Loading mesh geometry

I wrote an OBJ parser and used the parsed geometry to prepare vertex and normal buffers for WebGL2. The positions describe the mesh, while the normals give its surfaces a direction for lighting calculations.

Shading the surfaces

I implemented Phong lighting in the fragment shader so lighting could be evaluated across each visible surface during interactive rendering.
