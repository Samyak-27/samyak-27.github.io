Pavement test track drawings

I developed scaled AutoCAD drawings for the UC Pavement Research Center’s 2026 test track, covering research test sections, drainage, and existing site infrastructure.

Organization
UC Pavement Research Center
Project year
2026
Tools
Autodesk AutoCAD

Laying out the test sections

I developed scaled plan views to communicate how the pavement test sections fit within the research site. I coordinated revisions against the research team’s requirements and the infrastructure already in place.

Working across plans and sections

The plan views showed the site layout from above. I produced cross-sections to show the pavement structure and added drainage layouts and infrastructure details to document how the track related to the surrounding site.

Updating the drawing set

I carried forward the documentation from previous test-track designs and updated title blocks, layers, and annotations across the AutoCAD sheets. The resulting drawing set brought the track layout, drainage, and construction details together for review.
