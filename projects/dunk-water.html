Dunk water detection system

An Arduino-based controller that detects standing water and automatically activates a drainage pump.

Controller
Arduino, C/C++
Sensing and actuation
Float sensors, relay-controlled pump
Mechanical design
CAD enclosure, integrated power distribution

Detecting standing water

I used float sensors as inputs to the Arduino. The embedded C/C++ logic monitored those inputs and used their state to decide when to activate the pump.

Switching the pump

A relay connected the controller’s output to the pump circuit. I integrated power distribution for the electronics and pump and accounted for voltage regulation and electrical noise from relay switching.

Designing the enclosure

I used CAD to design a waterproof enclosure for the electronics. The enclosure and power integration were part of the same design, with outdoor exposure informing how I packaged the controller.
