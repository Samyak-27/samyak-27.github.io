Supporting research test systems

I maintain and troubleshoot MTS load frames and equipment used in ASTM-based tests. The work spans the load frame and its fixtures, the transducers that measure movement, and the hardware that collects their signals. I also support motors and variable-frequency drives used by lab equipment.

Checking the measurement chain

During a transducer connectivity check, I used the USB interface and its vendor utility to confirm a live reading from the device. That confirmed the instrument and interface were communicating and gave me a starting point for diagnosing the connection to the lab application.

Adapting equipment to new tests

When a test needs a new mechanical interface, I design fixtures and sensor mounts, specify the parts for fabrication, and coordinate revisions with the machine shop. I also coordinate equipment service and instrumentation upgrades with vendors and research staff.
