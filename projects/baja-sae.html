Design validation

Our team designs and builds an off-road vehicle for the Baja SAE competition. I coordinated design validation using SolidWorks and Ansys and helped bring individual subsystem designs together at the vehicle level.

Fabrication and integration

I oversaw fabrication work involving CNC machining, welding, and composite manufacturing. I coordinated across design and shop work so parts could be made and integrated with the rest of the vehicle.
