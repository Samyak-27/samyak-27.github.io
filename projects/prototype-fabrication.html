Additive manufacturing

Sensor fixture prototyping

From CAD to print preparation and completed PETG holder batches for lab fixtures and sensor hardware.

Organization
UC Pavement Research Center
Filament workflow
Bambu Studio, X1 Carbon, PETG
Resin preparation
PreForm, Form 3+, Black V4.1 and Rigid 4000

Preparing holder and clip batches

I sliced fixture parts in Bambu Studio and arranged holder and clip batches on the X1 Carbon build plates. One completed run covered thirteen additional holders across four plates, with three holder variants organized in the batch.

Making changes after print failures

I diagnosed failed prints and revised the setup before rerunning jobs. I adjusted part orientation, supports, and brims to address printability, and iterated infill and part placement to balance material use and build time with part requirements.

Preparing resin trials and fit checks

For the Form 3+, I prepared trial layouts and supports in PreForm for Black V4.1 and Rigid 4000 resin. The clip design still needed a fit trial, and the drill guides needed the sensor needle spacing confirmed.
