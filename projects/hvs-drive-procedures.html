HVS drive recovery procedures

I drafted drive-failure and disk-recovery procedures for Heavy Vehicle Simulator workstations, with drive checks, training steps, and supervisor review.

Organization
UC Pavement Research Center
Deliverables
Draft system-drive and GNU ddrescue procedures
Review steps
Drive identification, training checks, supervisor signoff

Documenting two recovery paths

I drafted one procedure for responding to a failed HVS system drive and a separate procedure for recovering readable data with GNU ddrescue. Each document identifies the intended recovery task and the checks that an authorized operator should complete before beginning.

Building in verification and review

I included steps for checking the identity of the source and destination drives, along with a training checklist and a place for supervisor review and signoff. The checklists give a reviewer a way to walk through the decisions before an operator works on a research workstation.

Connecting the procedures to a test lab

In a related portable Windows lab, I exercised post-recovery verification using synthetic data acquisition records. I used record sequences to check for gaps and SHA-256 checksums to verify file integrity.
