About

From sensors to software

I’m a computer science and engineering student at UC Davis and a Lead Technician at the UC Pavement Research Center. My work spans the full path from setting up an instrument to writing the software that makes sense of what it measures.

I’ve also worked on the infrastructure around experiments, including equipment service tools, calibration records, and recovery procedures that help research teams keep their systems running.
